# Level Editor Privacy Policy

Level Editor is a desktop tool for creating and editing 2D game maps. You can use the editor without a Level Editor account. The editor does not include advertising or usage analytics.

## Project files and settings

Maps, tilesets, and exported files are read and written in locations you select. Level Editor does not send project files to the developer. To reopen a project, the app may store its path and the permission granted through the macOS file picker. The app also stores settings, recent items, diagnostic logs, and recovery information on your Mac.

Project files and backups remain in their saved locations until you remove them. You can remove recent items from the home screen. You can delete exported files, backups, and log files in Finder. To remove locally stored preferences, recovery data, and saved folder access, quit the app and remove its local application data. If you have questions about removing that data, use the support link below.

## Online help and support

Opening the Help panel may fetch video thumbnails from YouTube. These HTTPS requests disclose your IP address and ordinary connection details to YouTube. The thumbnail requests do not include your projects or settings. The map editing features work without the thumbnail service.

Documentation, videos, and support links may open a site in your browser. Those sites handle the information you choose to share with them under their own policies. If you use the public issue tracker, the information you post is visible to other visitors.

Questions about this policy or privacy requests can be sent through the [Level Editor support page](https://github.com/bugs1111/LevelEditor/issues).
